============================================================
STEP 3 — how to trust the CA on the phone:
============================================================
Android: adb push ./ca.crt /sdcard/iotyk_ca.crt
         Settings → Security → Install certificate → CA certificate
iOS:     AirDrop or email ca.crt → tap install
         Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → Enable

============================================================
STEP 4 — exact instructions for pasting the EMQX CA certificate:
============================================================
1. Log into EMQX Cloud console
2. Go to: Deployment → Overview → Connection guide
3. Download the CA certificate (emqxsl-ca.crt)
4. Open in text editor, copy everything from -----BEGIN to END-----
5. Paste into EMQX_MQTT_CA_CERT in certificates.h replacing the
   PASTE_EMQX_CA_CERTIFICATE_HERE placeholder
Rules:
  - Keep -----BEGIN CERTIFICATE----- and -----END CERTIFICATE-----
  - No leading spaces on cert lines
  - No extra blank lines inside the cert block
  - The )EOF"; closing must stay on its own line
